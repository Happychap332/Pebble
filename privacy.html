Privacy

The short version

Your planner stays on your device. We don't have a database of users, we don't track you, and we don't sell anything about you.

What stays on your device

Your account (name, username and a protected version of your password), tasks, calendar, notes, flashcards, topics, focus history and settings are all saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to us.

What leaves your device

  • Fonts. Pebble loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when the font files load.
  • Hosting. Pebble is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Notifications and read-aloud

Notifications are created by your own browser. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voices.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Delete account, or clear this site's data in your browser. Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing else to delete on our side.
